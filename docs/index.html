04 / WHAT THE DATA CAN SUPPORT

Audit before interpretation.

Missing observations stay missing. Outcomes, timing and exclusion rules are explicit.

01

Price definition

Midpoint of valid YES bid and ask at the last completed minute bar before each cutoff. Quotes must be inside (0,1), uncrossed, and no more than 10¢ apart. Maximum bar lag: 120 seconds. Bar lag does not measure the age of the underlying resting quote.

02

Outcome definition

One home-team contract per event. Both team markets must have complementary binary settlements at $0 and $1. Voids, special settlements and ambiguous mappings are excluded.

03

Chronological evaluation

Split distinct UTC game dates 60% / 20% / 20%. Purge training outcomes unresolved before the earliest evaluation cutoff. Isotonic requires at least 1,000 training games. No test-set tuning.

04

Limits of the evidence

A capped recent settled cohort, not a full season or random sample. Scheduled starts may have been revised. Regular-season/playoff differences and recurring-team dependence can affect generalization. No claims about executable returns.

Snapshot exclusions

Counts change with the selected horizon. Collection exclusions occur before this stage.
